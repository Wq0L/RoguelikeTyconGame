Harvest Comic TR is a modified companion font derived from Lilita One.
Original copyright: Copyright (c) 2011 Juan Montoreano (juan@remolacha.biz).
Original reserved font name: Lilita.

This derivative is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see HarvestComicTR-OFL.txt.
The family and PostScript names are changed to Harvest Comic TR / HarvestComicTR-Regular.
The original LilitaOne-Regular.ttf is retained unchanged.

Modifications: added Gbreve, gbreve, Idotaccent, Scedilla and scedilla.
Existing G/g/I/S/s outlines, advance widths, lowercase i dot and cedilla are reused;
a rounded breve is added. The companion TMP font uses matching 90pt sampling,
12px padding, scale 1 and a pre-baked static SDF atlas.
Reproduction source: Tools/build-turkish-comic-font.py (requires fonttools).
